Tips for a better result
  • Use a sharp, evenly lit face photo looking toward the camera.
  • Choose a video where the target face stays visible and is not heavily covered.
  • Large head turns, profile views, hands over the face and strong lighting changes can cause artifacts.
  • For best alignment, use a face photo with a similar head angle to the video.
